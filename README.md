## 🇨🇳 中文

按新人故事与照片，分十四步做一支婚礼纪念视频，交付文案、旁白、外部生图提示词、视频包、配乐与字幕成片。每轮固定报进度，格式如【7/14｜等待图片回传｜还剩 7 步】，随后给出本步已交付、你现在只需要做什么、做完发回什么、确认后下一步，通常一轮最多三个操作，不让用户自己猜流程。所有生图只在外部对话里由用户完成，本 Skill 提供参考图映射与完整生图词，并等待实际文件回传；事实全部来自采集卡与确认后回填的补充，只允许语言、构图、色光、节奏、微表情等不改变事件意义的调整，不新增经历、关键物品、对白或人物关系。新单直接发故事采集卡（电脑/手机/文字版），续做则读 PROJECT_STATE.json 从待办继续，不重启采集、不串单。适合接新单或续做已有订单，不用于独立文案撰写或普通剪辑。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 新单：故事采集卡（电脑/手机/文字版）拿到新人原文即可开做
- 续做：读 PROJECT_STATE.json 从待办继续，不重启采集、不串单
- 每轮固定引导：本步已交付 / 你只需做 / 发回什么 / 下一步
- 文案与旁白：先完整旁白，再由声音与片长决定分镜
- 生图提示词 + 参考图映射，等用户回传真实图片再进下一步
- 成片交付：视频包 + 配乐 + 字幕，中英双语入口与同一套 14 步门控

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns a couple's story and photos into a wedding keepsake video across fourteen guided steps, delivering copy, narration, external image-generation prompts, a video package, music and a subtitled final cut. Every round reports progress in a fixed header such as 7/14 waiting on image handoff, then states what was delivered, what you need to do now, what to send back and what comes next — usually three actions or fewer, so the user never has to decide the workflow. All image generation happens outside, done by the user; the skill supplies reference-image mappings and complete prompts and waits for real files to come back. Facts come only from the intake card and confirmed follow-ups, permitting changes to language, framing, light, pacing and micro-expression but never new events, key objects, dialogue or relationships. New orders start from the story card (desktop, mobile or text); repeat orders resume from PROJECT_STATE.json instead of re-running intake. Not for standalone copywriting or ordinary editing.

### ✨ What it does

- New order: a story intake card for desktop, mobile or plain text, ready to build from the couple's own words
- Repeat order: resume from PROJECT_STATE.json without restarting intake or mixing up orders
- Fixed round script: delivered, what you do now, what to send back, what is next
- Copy and narration: finish the full narration first, then let real audio and target length set the shots
- Image prompts plus reference-image mapping, waiting for real files before the next step
- Final handoff: video package, music and subtitles, with bilingual entry and the same 14 gated steps
